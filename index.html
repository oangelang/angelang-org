Nature is why I think about AI and climate together.

These ecosystems shaped how I think about responsible technology. AI can help us monitor reefs, track species, and model emissions. It also consumes energy and resources. Both things are true, and both need measurement. I dive to see how healthy the ocean actually is.

The dots that have been drifting across the page come together into a whale shark.

The work I want to do goes deep.

Depth means evidence you can trace. Like a whale shark's spots, every case here is its own pattern, and I keep the sources next to it.

Whale sharks are the largest fish in the sea, and they have been tracked diving more than a kilometer down. Each one wears a pattern of spots that belongs to it alone. Researchers use those spots to tell individuals apart and follow them across oceans.

They are endangered, and they feed on the plankton that the whole ocean depends on. That is how I think about the communities I work with. Every dot is different, but whole when all connected together.
